01 /about.mdBiography

# profile

Born in Fuzhou, China, I returned home at the end of 2025 after several years of study and research in Denmark. I’m now an Associate Research Professor at Fuzhou University’s College of Computer and Data Science, where I lead the Data Intelligence and Interdisciplinary Information Group. I’m also a member of the Fujian Key Laboratory of Network Computing and Intelligent Information Processing, mentored by Professor Xing Chen.

# research

I work on time series and spatio-temporal data, developing efficient methods for learning from incomplete, interconnected, and evolving data. I’m also interested in GeoAI and AI for science and health.

# education

I earned my B.S. from UESTC in 2018 and my M.S. from Sichuan University in 2021, advised by Professor Jiancheng Lv. I then moved to Denmark for my Ph.D. at Aalborg University, which I completed in early 2025 under the supervision of Professors Christian S. Jensen (Member of Academia Europaea; ACM/IEEE Fellow), Dalin Zhang, and Huan Li. I also worked as a Research Fellow with Professor Hua Lu.

# visits

During my Ph.D., I also spent time at NTNU with Professor Xu Cheng, and at UNSW Sydney with Professors Lina Yao and Dong Gong.
