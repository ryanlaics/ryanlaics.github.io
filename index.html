Born in Fuzhou, China 🇨🇳, I’m now back in my hometown after studying in Denmark 🇩🇰. I'm now an Associate Research Professor at Fuzhou University's College of Computer and Data Science. I lead the Data Intelligence and Interdisciplinary Information Group (DIG@FZU). I'm also a member of the Fujian Key Laboratory of Network Computing and Intelligent Information Processing, mentored by Professor Xing Chen.

I study time series and spatio-temporal data. I develop efficient methods for learning from incomplete, interconnected, and changing data. My broader interests include GeoAI and AI for science and health.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2018, then my M.S. in Computer Science from Sichuan University in 2021, advised by Professor Jiancheng Lv. I moved to Denmark for my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Aalborg University, completed in early 2025 under Professors Christian S. Jensen (Member of Academia Europaea, ACM Fellow, and IEEE Fellow), Dalin Zhang, and Huan Li. I also served as a Research Fellow with Professor Hua Lu and, in 2024, a Junior Visiting Research Fellow at the University of New South Wales with Professors Lina Yao and Dong Gong.
