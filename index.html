01 /about.md

Born in Fuzhou, China, I returned home in late 2025 after several years in Denmark. I’m now an Associate Research Professor at Fuzhou University’s College of Computer and Data Science, where I lead the Data Intelligence and Interdisciplinary Information Group (DIG@FZU).

I develop efficient methods for learning from time series and spatio-temporal data, especially when series are correlated, observations are missing, or patterns change over time. My broader interests include GeoAI and AI for science and health.

I’m a member of the Fujian Key Laboratory of Network Computing and Intelligent Information Processing, where I’m mentored by Professor Xing Chen. My research is supported by China’s MOE National Overseas Talent Recruitment Program and the NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category C).

I studied Computer Science in China, earning my B.S. at UESTC in 2018 and my M.S. at Sichuan University in 2021. My master’s advisor was Professor Jiancheng Lv.

In early 2025, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Aalborg University (Denmark) under the supervision of Professors Christian S. Jensen (Member of Academia Europaea; ACM/IEEE Fellow), Dalin Zhang, and Huan Li. I also worked as a Research Fellow with Professor Hua Lu.
