About

I am an Associate Research Professor in the College of Computer and Data Science, Fuzhou University, China. I am also a member of the Fujian Key Laboratory of Network Computing and Intelligent Information Processing, where I work closely with Prof. Xing Chen under his mentorship. I was selected for China's Ministry of Education Overseas Talent Program (教育部海外引才计划) in 2025. I currently lead the Data Intelligence and Interdisciplinary Information Group (DIG@FZU).

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Aalborg University (AAU), Denmark, in early 2025, where I also served as a Research Fellow. At AAU, I was advised by Professor Christian S. Jensen, a Member of Academia Europaea and an ACM and IEEE Fellow, together with Professors Dalin Zhang, Huan Li, and Hua Lu.

I was also a Junior Visiting Research Fellow at the University of New South Wales (UNSW) in 2024, under the supervision of Professors Lina Yao and Dong Gong. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Sichuan University (SCU) in 2021 under the supervision of Prof. Jiancheng Lv, and my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in 2018.

Research Areas

01

Spatio-Temporal Data Mining

Representation learning, similarity modeling, retrieval, and prediction for trajectories, events, and other structured spatio-temporal data.

02

Geospatial Artificial Intelligence

Data-driven methods for understanding geographic processes, human mobility, and place-based patterns across space and time.

03

Efficient Deep Learning

Resource-aware model design, training, and inference for deep learning systems that must be both effective and deployable.

04

AI for Science & Health

Machine learning for scientific and healthcare data, including interdisciplinary applications in dental medicine and environmental systems.
